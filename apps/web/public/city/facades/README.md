The main JPEG is an unchanged copy of the published, privacy-blurred image.
Two additional unchanged standard-resolution Panoramax photographs by Lejun,
also under CC-BY-SA-4.0, provide a clearer tower side and the reference for
the grey block's reconstructed eastern front:

- https://explore.panoramax.fr/#focus=pic&pic=edfe5c1b-3de1-4a51-87d8-712de9d1131d
- https://explore.panoramax.fr/#focus=pic&pic=bbf18b7c-94a6-4969-9cf5-6be5ca50dc98

Each source URL, SHA-256, dimensions and capture timestamp are recorded in the
adjacent reference manifest. The browser resamples the main texture to a
maximum 4096 px wide and the additional textures to 2048 px for bounded GPU memory.

Adaptations: three selected upper wall faces on two matched IGN buildings receive
the photographs through manually registered equirectangular UV coordinates.
The grey eastern front is reconstructed as geometry using four observed upper
storeys, asymmetric window/shutter bays, balconies, rails, floor bands and the
visible business names, avoiding foreground objects in the texture. A third,
pale-blue retail building is reconstructed from visible colors, canopy,
ribbon windows and ground-floor glazing; its dimensions and window spacing
are estimates. These photographic facade adaptations and reference profiles
are provided under CC-BY-SA-4.0. IGN geometry and OSM street data retain their
separate original licences; this statement does not relicense those datasets.

No Google imagery, generated facade images, or invented business logos are used.
Occluded and unobserved faces are not photo reconstructions. Registration is
approximate, and photographed lighting, shadows and some occlusions remain.
